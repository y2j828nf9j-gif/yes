Meet your coach

I'm Leo, and I coach you to understand your training.

I'm a NASM-qualified personal trainer based at PureGym Giltbrook. I started KnowledgeGainzz because most people don't fail for lack of effort. They fail because nobody explained the plan to them.

Every session, programme and nutrition target comes with the reasoning behind it. You'll leave stronger, and you'll know how to keep progressing on your own.

How it works

From first message to first session in four steps

  1. 01

    Submit your enquiry

    Complete the online form: contact details, health questionnaire (PAR-Q), experience, goals, activity levels and nutrition. It takes about 5 minutes.

  2. 02

    Get your checklist

    You'll get an email straight away with a copy of your answers and a checklist of anything else I need before we start.

  3. 03

    Free consultation

    I'll contact you within 24 hours, by email or social media, whichever you prefer, to book a free consultation at the gym.

  4. 04

    Start training

    You get a programme built from your answers, and I explain every part of it.
